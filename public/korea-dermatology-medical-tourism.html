Who this page is for

Foreign patients who want to ask about Korean dermatology or skin-care options but need help organizing non-diagnostic consultation information first. Use this page to prepare questions, documents, language needs, and timing details for Korea dermatology consultation.

Consultation or service process

  1. Describe the skin concern, affected area, timing, and whether the question is medical, cosmetic, or K-beauty related.
  2. Collect optional photos, prior treatment history, medication or allergy notes, and preferred consultation language.
  3. A coordinator reviews missing context and may prepare a clinic inquiry only with the patient’s consent.
  4. After clinic feedback, appointment timing, interpreter support, and travel movement can be discussed without guaranteeing outcomes.

Information to prepare

  • Clear photos taken in similar lighting, with no filters, only if the patient chooses to share them.
  • Previous skin treatments, laser or injection history, product reactions, medications, allergies, and sensitive-skin notes.
  • Main goal such as acne, pigmentation, redness, texture, scar concern, or general K-beauty skin-care guidance.
  • Preferred clinic area, travel dates, recovery-time concerns, and whether interpretation is needed.

Why costs can vary

Dermatology-related costs may vary by the consultation result, skin condition review, possible tests, device or procedure scope, product needs, number of visits, and clinic policy. Online guidance cannot confirm a final price.

Important cautions

  • AI or photo review is only a reference and cannot diagnose skin disease.
  • Do not hide previous procedures, medication, allergies, or recent reactions from licensed professionals.
  • Recovery time and suitability may differ by individual skin condition and clinic review.
  • Avoid choosing a procedure only from before-and-after images or social media claims.

FAQ

Can I ask about possible skin procedures before visiting Korea?

Yes. You can organize questions about concerns and possible consultation topics, but suitability must be reviewed by licensed professionals.

What kind of photos are helpful?

Clear, unfiltered photos in consistent lighting may help prepare an inquiry, but sharing them is optional and consent-based.

Should I mention previous lasers or injections?

Yes. Prior procedures, product reactions, medication, and allergies can affect how a clinic reviews the inquiry.

Why can dermatology costs vary?

Costs may differ by skin review, device or procedure scope, visit count, products, clinic policy, and aftercare needs.

Is this a dermatology diagnosis?

No. This page supports pre-consultation organization and coordination only; diagnosis is made by licensed medical professionals.
