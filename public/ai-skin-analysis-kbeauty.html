Who this page is for

K-beauty curious visitors who want to try an easy reference tool before deciding what questions to ask in a consultation request. Use this page to prepare questions, documents, language needs, and timing details for AI skin analysis for K-beauty reference.

Consultation or service process

  1. Use AI Skin Analysis only as a reference experience and read the non-diagnostic notice before uploading anything.
  2. Review the output as a way to organize skin-care interests, not as medical confirmation.
  3. If you want human support, send the reference result with your goals, history, and language preference.
  4. A coordinator can help turn the reference into consultation questions for licensed professionals or relevant clinics.

Information to prepare

  • A clear photo only if you consent to using it for the AI reference experience.
  • Your main K-beauty interests such as moisture, tone, texture, acne concern, redness, or product guidance.
  • Past skin reactions, current products, medication, and whether you want clinic consultation or general K-beauty guidance.
  • Preferred language, country, travel timeline, and whether you want to continue to a consultation form.

Why costs can vary

AI Skin Analysis may be a starting reference, but costs after consultation can vary by clinic review, skin-care program, device or product discussion, interpreter time, and whether an in-person visit is needed.

Important cautions

  • AI output is not a diagnosis and should not replace licensed medical advice.
  • Do not make medical or travel decisions based only on an AI reference.
  • Use only photos you are comfortable sharing for the stated purpose.
  • If symptoms are severe, painful, or rapidly changing, seek licensed medical care directly.

FAQ

No. It is a non-diagnostic K-beauty reference tool for organizing questions before consultation.

Yes. You may share it as reference context, along with your concerns, history, and language preference.

Only upload a photo if you understand the purpose and agree to use it for the reference experience.

What is the next step after AI analysis?

You can continue to the consultation form or ask for human coordination if you want organized guidance.
