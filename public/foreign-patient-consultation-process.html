Who this page is for

Foreign patients who feel unsure about what happens after they submit a Korea medical travel inquiry. Use this page to prepare questions, documents, language needs, and timing details for foreign patient consultation process in Korea.

Consultation or service process

  1. Submit basic inquiry details such as medical field, concern, country, language, contact, and preferred visit period.
  2. A coordinator checks missing information and may ask follow-up questions before any hospital communication.
  3. With consent, organized information can be prepared for a licensed medical institution or clinic contact point.
  4. After feedback, interpretation, appointment timing, travel support, and follow-up communication are coordinated step by step.

Information to prepare

  • Identity and contact information, preferred language, country of residence, and messenger or email that is checked regularly.
  • Medical concern, prior diagnosis or consultation, medication, allergies, documents, and relevant photos if willingly shared.
  • Preferred city, visit dates, companion needs, interpretation requests, and flexibility for schedule changes.
  • Questions you want answered before deciding whether to continue with hospital communication.

Why costs can vary

Costs may differ at each stage because the final medical field, tests, appointment type, interpreter hours, travel support, accommodation, and follow-up needs are not known at first inquiry.

Important cautions

  • Do not expect a final diagnosis or price at the intake stage.
  • Answer follow-up questions accurately so the hospital inquiry is not based on incomplete information.
  • Consent should be clear before personal medical information is shared with any third party.
  • Schedule coordination may change after licensed professionals review the case.

Legal notice

VR MEDI TOUR & HOME is not a hospital or medical institution. This service provides pre-consultation, interpretation, coordination, travel support, and information guidance. AI Skin Analysis is not a medical diagnosis. Final diagnosis, treatment plan, and price must be confirmed by licensed medical professionals and medical institutions.

FAQ

What happens after I submit an inquiry?

A coordinator reviews the information, checks missing details, and organizes the next communication step.

Will my information be sent to a hospital immediately?

No. Information should be organized and shared only with appropriate consent and purpose.

Can interpretation be arranged during the process?

Language needs can be discussed during coordination, depending on timing and availability.

Why can the plan change after hospital review?

The institution may request different documents, tests, timing, or consultation steps after reviewing the inquiry.

Is the coordinator making medical decisions?

No. The coordinator organizes information and logistics; licensed professionals make medical decisions.
