Who this page is for

International patients who need a step-by-step process, preparation checklist, and multilingual support before Korean medical appointments. This page is also useful for families comparing Korea medical tourism, K-beauty, Busan, Gangnam, and Vietnam-related support options.

Consultation or service process

  1. Send a free pre-consultation request with goals, country, language, schedule, and contact details.
  2. A human coordinator reviews the inquiry and organizes missing information.
  3. With your consent, information may be prepared for communication with relevant licensed medical institutions.
  4. Interpretation, appointment timing, travel support, and follow-up coordination can be discussed.

Information to prepare

  • Preferred language: Korean, English, Vietnamese, or another available language.
  • Current concerns, previous diagnosis if any, allergies, medication, and visit timeline.
  • Photos or documents only when you choose to share them for consultation preparation.
  • Travel dates, preferred area such as Busan or Gangnam, and budget range for planning.

Why costs can vary

Medical tourism costs can vary because diagnosis, test results, treatment scope, facility policy, anesthesia or medication needs, interpreter time, transport, accommodation, recovery schedule, and aftercare can differ. Online guidance is only preliminary; final price must be confirmed by licensed medical professionals and medical institutions.

Important cautions

  • Do not travel based only on online estimates or AI output.
  • Bring accurate medical history and ask licensed professionals about risks, alternatives, and recovery time.
  • Confirm visa, travel insurance, medication rules, and emergency contact options before travel.
  • Use privacy-aware document sharing and avoid sending sensitive information without consent and purpose clarity.
