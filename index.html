Cost Guidance Principle

  • Fixed prices are not guaranteed before hospital consultation.
  • Estimated costs may vary depending on diagnosis, test results, treatment scope, stay duration, and hospital policy.
  • After medical institution review, estimated ranges can be explained clearly.

Hospital Partnership

  • Foreign patient inquiry organization
  • Multilingual pre-consultation preparation
  • Patient document checklist
  • Preferred language and schedule collection
  • Hospital contact point support
  • Referral preparation without medical decision claims

Privacy & Medical Information Protection

  • Photos, symptoms, documents, and contact details are used only for consultation preparation and hospital communication.
  • Information is not shared with third parties without consent.
  • When hospital review is needed, permission must be requested before forwarding documents.
